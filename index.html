Install help and FAQ

Mac: "Sori can't be opened" or "can't check the developer"
  1. Open Sori-mac.dmg and drag Sori into Applications.
  2. Open Sori. When macOS warns you, click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Sori.
  4. Allow the Microphone. Then turn Sori on under Accessibility and Input Monitoring in the same Privacy & Security page, and reopen Sori.

The warning appears because Sori is an independent app that isn't registered with Apple's paid developer program yet.

Windows: "Windows protected your PC"

Click More info, then Run anyway. This shows up for new apps from small developers. Sori installs for your user only and doesn't need an administrator password. After installing, look for the microphone icon at the bottom-right of the taskbar (click ^ if it's hidden).

Why is the first start slow?

The first time, Sori downloads its speech model (about 500 MB). After that it works offline and starts quickly.

What languages does it support?

Pick one in Sori's Language menu: English, Chinese (Mandarin), Hindi, Spanish, French, Arabic, Bengali, Portuguese, Russian, Urdu, Indonesian, German, Japanese, Marathi, Telugu, Turkish, Tamil, Vietnamese, Korean or Italian. Auto-detect handles about 100 languages if you switch between them. The first time you choose a language other than English, Sori downloads its multilingual speech model (about 500 MB).

What's the optional AI cleanup?

Basic cleanup is always on and free. For smarter grammar fixes, choose Cleanup: built-in AI in Sori's menu: a small AI model runs inside Sori on your own computer, free and offline, with nothing else to install (it downloads once, about 1 GB). Or paste your own Claude API key in Sori's menu for the sharpest rewrite. With the Claude option, only the text (never your audio) is sent to Anthropic, under your own account.

How does Sori Pro work?

Buy a plan above and your licence key arrives by email. In Sori's menu choose Enter Pro licence key... and paste it. To move Pro to another computer, choose Remove Pro licence on the old one first. Pro checks in with the licence server every few days and keeps working offline for up to 30 days.

How do I uninstall?

Mac: drag Sori from Applications to the Trash. Windows: Settings > Apps > Sori > Uninstall.
